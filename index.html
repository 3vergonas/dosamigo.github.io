Who wants to be king?!

Welcome to Imps' Reign, an ever-evolving tabletop experience from Long & Lost Games where you don't just play the game—you build it. Download and 3D-print your own modular board tiles, terrain, and customizable miniatures featuring interchangeable heads, torsos, and legs. Whether you are executing a solo rescue mission, clashing in a party-sized tactical war, or diving into the lore via our companion manga, Imps' Reign gives you the blueprints to forge your own adventures. New designs drop monthly!

ABOUT IMPS' REIGN

A Smarter System For Tabletop Gameplay

Tabletop Combat, Engineered.

Imps' Reign is designed specifically for highly modular integration—bold forms, clean lines, and complex loadouts. What others see as difficult, we've engineered for from the start.

Precision-Sculpted Modules.

High-quality digital assets are formatted in a controlled environment, ready to be delivered and printed right at your desk—bringing precision, predictability, and efficiency to every figure.

Faster by Design.

While traditional wargaming prep moves step by step, our modular limb and neck seams run in parallel—compressing prep timelines and accelerating tabletop deployment.
